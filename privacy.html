The Couture - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

The Couture respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with The Couture through our website, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, or related services.

Information We May Collect

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery details, product preferences, and messages sent to us.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to customer questions and requests.
  • To provide product, order, delivery, and support services.
  • To improve our products and customer experience.
  • To operate our WhatsApp and social-media communication services.

WhatsApp and Meta Services

When you communicate with The Couture through WhatsApp or Meta platforms, your information may be processed by Meta and the relevant platform according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate our services, process orders, provide delivery, or comply with applicable law.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, legal requirements, dispute resolution, and legitimate business needs.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact The Couture at the email address associated with our business.
