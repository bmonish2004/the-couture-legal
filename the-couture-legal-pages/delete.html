The Couture - User Data Deletion

Request deletion of your data

If you want The Couture to delete personal information associated with your interactions with us, please contact us through our official customer-support channel and clearly state that you want your data deleted.

Please include the phone number or email address associated with your request so that we can identify the relevant records. We may need to verify ownership before processing the request.

What happens next?

We will review the request and delete information that we are permitted or required to delete, subject to applicable legal, accounting, security, and fraud-prevention requirements.

Contact

Contact The Couture through the official contact method associated with your business account.
